How to use profiles and priority classes

Choose a profile for your business goal

Each profile answers a different commercial question, so select the one that matches the campaign or product decision you are making.

Balanced
Use for general account planning when value, DeFi usage, and recent engagement all matter.
Activity-led
Use for launches, beta invitations, cross-sell, and adoption campaigns. It surfaces wallets already active on-chain and more likely to try something now.
Capital-led
Use for high-value acquisition, VIP or BD outreach, and capital migration. It surfaces wallets with more capital to deploy, even when they are less active.

Choose the right outreach motion

The priority class suggests how much effort to invest and what kind of engagement is most appropriate now.

Priority outreach
Best near-term sales or BD targets. Assign an owner and use personalized outreach.
Strategic nurture
Valuable accounts that are not showing much readiness now. Keep them warm with tailored content and watch for a new activity trigger.
Automated marketing
Engaged wallets that do not yet justify costly one-to-one outreach. Use scalable onboarding, education, and campaign journeys.
Low priority
Avoid spending sales time for now. Keep them in low-cost communications and reassess when their activity or capital changes.
